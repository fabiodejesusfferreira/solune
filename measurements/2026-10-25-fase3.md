## What I saw

- **Fullscreen pause works.** The window opened at 16:54:12 local time (the script logs whole seconds). The log shows `tela cheia: Some(true)` at 19:54:13.003Z, and `status` 3 s later gave `motivo da pausa: fullscreen`.
- **The release happened about 60 s later:** `decodificador liberado depois da pausa longa` at 19:55:13.892Z.
- **Playback resumed** when the window closed (16:56:22 local; `tela cheia: Some(false)` at 19:56:22.970Z).
- **Resident memory went up after the release and did not come back down.** VmRSS went from 149.4 MiB (paused) to 200.7 MiB (released), and it stayed at 200.7 MiB playing again and 201.0 MiB after the second release. Releasing the decoder freed no RAM here; it added about 51 MiB that wasn't returned.
- **Video memory while paused went up, not down.** "memória de vídeo" was 103.9 MiB playing, 148.4 MiB paused, 128.1 MiB released and 103.8 MiB playing again. The released value is still above the playing value.
- **The first `usage` after the release (discarded as the sampler start) showed `cpu: 3,2%`.** That window covers the moment of the release.
- **User pause with the desktop visible** (`/tmp/solune-liberado.png`, after 65 s of `pause`, with `decodificador liberado: sim`): the background shows a still frame of the video, with the icons on top. It isn't black and it isn't the normal wallpaper. I showed the desktop with `wmctrl -k on` and then restored the windows with `wmctrl -k off`.
- **After `resume`** (`/tmp/solune-retomado.png`): a different frame of the video, with the icons on top. A capture 1 s later (`/tmp/solune-retomado-2.png`) differs in 2,026,330 pixels (`compare -metric AE`), so the video is moving.
- **Shutdown is clean.** After `quit`, pgrep is empty, `$XDG_RUNTIME_DIR/solune/` is empty and no frame-*.png was left.
- **Battery reading.** `BAT1` reports `status=Discharging` while `ADP1` reports `online=1`. The daemon logged `bateria: na tomada`, and `policy` printed `agora, na bateria: não`, which matches the adapter being plugged in. I didn't test unplugging the charger.
- **`policy` reports "não" for all three signals, never "não detectado".** The log shows that all three were read: `Some(false)` for fullscreen and lock, `na tomada` for the battery.
- **No WARN or ERROR lines in the log.**

## Depois da correção (b50d003)

Run on 2026-10-25 at 17:13–17:18 local time, release build of b50d003, throwaway config `/tmp/tmp.HHwWxA8krM`, `SOLUNE_RELEASE_AFTER_SECS=60`.

**This run was contaminated.** The Claude desktop window (0x7400004, `com.anthropic.Claude`) was in real fullscreen (`_NET_WM_STATE_FULLSCREEN`) during the test. The daemon detected it correctly (`tela cheia: Some(true)` at 20:13:48Z, before the `play`), which caused three problems:

- The "tocando" window was in fact paused by fullscreen: 0 fps and 0.07 % CPU.
- `wmctrl -k on` didn't hide the fullscreen window, so every screenshot shows the Claude window (or a file manager window) instead of the background. The pixel differences don't measure the video.
- In step 10 the detector was already `Some(true)` before `fullscreen.py` opened, so there was no transition, no pause and no release.

What is still valid: the release after the user pause, and playing again after `resume`.
